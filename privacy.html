Who we are

Identity Frontline (identityfrontline.com) is an independent publication on identity and access management, written by Syed Masood Raza Kazmi. For any privacy question or request, contact us through the Identity Frontline LinkedIn page or by replying to any newsletter email.

Newsletter

The newsletter is run on Substack. When you subscribe (on this site's embedded form or on Substack directly), your email address is collected and processed by Substack under Substack's privacy policy. You can unsubscribe at any time from the link in every email.

Job board

The job board is currently closed. When it is open, it works like this:

  • Email verification: to apply, you enter your email address and we send you a one-click confirmation link. Verified email addresses are stored so you do not have to verify again (the verification lasts up to 180 days in your browser).
  • Applications: when you apply, we store your name, email, optional phone number and LinkedIn URL, your note and your résumé file, together with the role you applied for and the time of the application.
  • Where it is stored: in Microsoft Azure Storage (tables and a private blob container) under our account. Confirmation and notification emails are sent through an email delivery provider (SendGrid).
  • Who sees it: your application is shared only with the hiring contact for the role you applied to.
  • How long we keep it: applications are deleted after [retention period], or sooner on request.

Cloud and Zero Trust assessments

The Cloud Assessments and Zero Trust assessment run entirely in your browser. You sign in with your own Microsoft account, and your browser reads your tenant's configuration directly from Microsoft using read-only permissions you consent to. No tenant data is sent to this website. Reports are generated in your browser and downloaded to your device. To show what changed between runs, a compact summary of each completed run (check results only, no tokens or raw tenant data) is kept in your browser's local storage, and you can clear it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Analytics and cookies

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It does not use cookies and does not track you across sites. Our hosting provider (Microsoft Azure) may set a technical load-balancing cookie (ARRAffinity) that is needed for the site to work. Embedded Substack sign-up forms are served by Substack and are subject to Substack's own policy.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you (for example a job application) by contacting us as described above. We will respond within a reasonable time.
