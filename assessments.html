Cloud security & compliance

Know your cloud posture.

Plan a focused, read-only assessment of your Microsoft 365 and Entra ID environment. Your tenant data stays on your machine.

Local-first by designNo tenant data passes through this website.

The runner will authenticate through Microsoft, collect only your chosen scope, and generate confidential reports locally.

What this does

A practical security snapshot—not a certification.

Select services and frameworks, review the read-only permissions needed, then prepare a local assessment package. Findings will map one technical condition to every selected compliance framework without duplicating the underlying evidence.

Read onlyNever changes policies, roles, users, or resources.
Least privilegePermissions are calculated from your selected scope.
Local reportsHTML, Excel, and CSV results remain on your device.
Scoped evidenceOnly selected service configuration is assessed.
