Cloud security & compliance

Know your cloud posture.

Assess your Microsoft 365 and Entra ID security in minutes. Sign in, consent to read-only access, and get a report — without installing anything.

Runs in your browserNo tenant data passes through this website.

Your browser reads your tenant directly from Microsoft using your own sign-in. Reports are generated here and downloaded to your machine.

What this does

A practical security snapshot—not a certification.

Choose the frameworks you care about, sign in to your tenant, and the assessment runs in this tab against Microsoft Graph. Each finding maps one technical condition to every selected compliance framework without duplicating the evidence.

Read onlyNever changes policies, roles, users, or resources.
Nothing to installWorks in any modern browser. No PowerShell, no agents.

Step 3

Review required permissions

Your browser will ask Microsoft for exactly these delegated, read-only permissions. Nothing more is requested, and application permissions are never used.

Your assessment requires read-only access.It reads configuration, evaluates it, and produces findings. It cannot modify policies, create users, assign roles, install agents, or change anything in your tenant.
PermissionTypeWhy it is neededRisk
Administrator consent is usually required. These are organisation-wide read permissions. If you are not a Global Administrator, sign in anyway — the next step will tell you which permissions still need an administrator, and you can run with reduced coverage.
Step 4

Connect to your tenant

Enter the tenant to assess, then sign in with an account in that tenant. Global Reader is enough for a complete run.

    Security & privacy

    Built around tenant trust.

    Authentication

    Microsoft sign-in only, using the standard browser flow (authorization code with PKCE). No passwords, secrets or tokens are ever entered on this site.

    Data handling

    Your browser calls Microsoft Graph directly. This site never receives your token, your tenant data, or your report. There is no server-side processing to audit because there is none.

    Transparent limits

    Results are a posture snapshot and remediation guide, not a certification or formal audit opinion. Coverage is stated on every report.
