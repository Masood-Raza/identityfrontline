The author

Masood Raza

Identity & access practitioner, focused on Microsoft Entra ID.

I'm Masood Raza (Syed Masood Raza Kazmi), an identity and access management practitioner with more than 24 years of hands-on experience in enterprise IT across North America, Europe and the Gulf region. Most of my work lives in Microsoft Entra ID: hybrid identity with Active Directory, Conditional Access and MFA, privileged access with PIM, single sign-on and SCIM provisioning to cloud apps, and automating the joiner-mover-leaver lifecycle.

I started Identity Frontline to do that work in the open. Every guide is a lab you can build yourself on free tiers, from an Entra ID and Active Directory homelab to Entra SSO for AWS and Microsoft Graph-driven leaver automation. The Cloud and Zero Trust assessments are free tools that run in your browser with read-only permissions and never send your tenant data to this site.

I'm a Microsoft Certified Trainer and hold the Identity and Access Administrator (SC-300) and Azure Solutions Architect Expert (AZ-305) certifications, among others, along with ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity. I enjoy explaining identity so that engineers can go and build it themselves.
